Dayflow HRMS

One place for people, presence and pay. Employee profiles, attendance, time-off approvals and payroll — with a dashboard your team will actually want to open.

🔒

Roles & Sign-up

Self registration with Employee ID, a strong password policy and e-mail verification. Two roles: Employee and Admin / HR Officer.

⏰

Attendance

One-click check-in / check-out, daily and weekly views, and automatic Present / Half-day / Absent / Leave statuses.

🌴

Time Off

Paid, sick and unpaid leave with balances, an approval workflow and comments on every decision.

💳

Payroll

Reusable salary structures, loss-of-pay from attendance, PDF payslips and read-only employee access.

Two dashboards, two jobs

Employee

  • Check in / out straight from the hero card
  • Seven-day attendance strip and monthly totals
  • Leave balances per type, with one-click requests
  • Recent activity feed and payslip shortcuts

Admin / HR Officer

  • Headcount, attendance rate and payroll KPIs
  • Approve or reject leave without leaving the page
  • 14-day attendance trend and department split
  • Quick switch to any employee's full record

Privacy by design

Record rules keep employees inside their own data: their attendance, their leave, their payslips. Field-level permissions let them maintain their address, phone numbers and profile picture — everything else stays with HR. Payroll is read-only for everyone but Admin / HR Officers.
